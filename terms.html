Terms and data

A plain-language summary of what PP Aqua stores and how the service behaves.

What this is

PP Aqua is a hobby project for turning children's coloured fish drawings into animated 3D fish. It is provided as-is and may change or become unavailable.

Accounts, codes, and passwords

  • There are no user accounts.
  • The aquarium code acts as the viewing link and allows adding fish and changing non-destructive settings.
  • The aquarium password protects destructive actions such as deleting fish, deleting the aquarium, renaming it, and changing the password.
  • If the code or password is lost, there is no account-based recovery mechanism.

What is stored

  • Uploaded fish drawings.
  • The aquarium name.
  • A scene preview image.
  • Custom aquarium backgrounds.
  • A salted scrypt password hash, never the plaintext password.

Application logging: the aquarium server does not intentionally store visitor IP addresses in aquarium data. A reverse proxy in front of it may create ordinary technical access logs, depending on its configuration.

Deletion

Deleted fish and aquariums are moved to server-side trash first so accidental deletion can be recovered. Trash is permanently purged after the configured retention period, 30 days by default.

User responsibility

The service is intended for children's fish drawings. Do not upload unlawful, abusive, or unrelated material. The operator may remove content or disable access when necessary to protect the service or its users.

Personal data

The service is designed to minimize personal data. It does not require an account, email address, or profile. A public deployment should provide a jurisdiction-appropriate privacy notice and a real contact address before accepting public users.

Fish models

The 3D fish models are separately licensed assets and are not part of the MIT-licensed application source.
